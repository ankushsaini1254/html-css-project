What is FX trading?

Forex trading or currency trading is the buying or selling of one country’s currency in exchange for another.

  • Forex is one of the most liquid markets in the world
  • It has a trading volume of $6 trillion per day
  • The US dollar is the most widely traded currency in the world

WHAT AFFECTS FOREX MARKET MOVEMENTS?

    Currency exchange rates can change due to several economic and financial factors.
  1. Interest Rates
    Central bank interest rate decisions can influence currency values.
  2. Inflation
    Changes in inflation can affect purchasing power and monetary policy.
  3. Economic Reports
    GDP, employment data, and other economic indicators can influence exchange rates.
  4. Political Events
    Political developments and geopolitical uncertainty can affect currency markets.
  5. Global Market Sentiment
    Investor confidence and global economic conditions can influence currency demand.

FOREX TRADING SESSIONS
